# Sinoog 2027 design context

The interface uses a dignified parish-festival direction: deep wine for ceremony and identity, warm gold for celebration, and paper cream for schedules and long-form information. Fraunces supplies editorial warmth; DM Sans keeps forms, navigation, and administration views clear.

The public site favors large date cues, simple event lists, clear publication states, and restrained cultural motifs. The committee workspace favors compact tables, status labels, and direct actions. Unconfirmed details must remain visibly marked as “to be announced.”
